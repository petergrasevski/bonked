Couldn’t connect locally

  1. Put the phone and laptop on the same main Wi-Fi. Guest, hotel and office networks may isolate devices.
  2. Allow local-network access if the browser or phone asks. If you denied it, change the site / browser permission in settings.
  3. Enable local-network access in your VPN, or disconnect it temporarily.
  4. On your own router, check “AP isolation” or “client isolation”. A different home router may work where a venue network doesn’t.
  5. Keep the host tab open and the laptop awake. Try a current Safari or Chrome browser.
